# 03 · Inquiry Scripts (30+ short replies — change the numbers, send)

> **Rules:** Clarify scope before quoting. Say no clearly when you can’t. Never promise outcomes platforms forbid.  
> Replace `[ ]` with your niche and prices. Save favorites in Notes.

---

## A. First reply (open + ask for the brief)

1. Hi — I take `[SKILL]` orders. For an accurate quote, please send: (1) use case (2) current assets (3) deadline (4) optional references.
2. Here. What’s the exact file/format you need delivered? I’ll confirm fit, then share timing and price.
3. Got it. I estimate by `[pages / images / words / features]`. Can you share volume + deadline?
4. Yes, I do this. Confirm: edit your existing file, or build from scratch? Pricing differs.
5. Hi. Three short lines are enough: what you want, what you already have, when you need it. I’ll reply with yes/no + quote.

---

## B. Dig for a clear brief (before naming a price)

6. Please complete: assets ready? must-keep content? hard “don’ts”? acceptance criteria (what “done” means)?
7. To avoid rework: `[N]` revision rounds included; mid-project topic/style swaps or big add-ons need a new quote — OK?
8. Priority check: speed or polish? I’ll recommend the matching tier.
9. Send one reference closest to your taste (someone else’s is fine) and note “like this / not like that.”
10. Is the deadline hard? If it must be `[tonight / tomorrow]`, I’ll check calendar before promising rush.

---

## C. Quoting (lock scope so you don’t fight later)

11. Based on your note, I’d suggest **Standard**: includes `[scope]`, `[N]` rounds, about `[X]` delivery, `$[__]`. Confirm and I’ll start after order/payment per platform.
12. For this volume: `$[__]` — delivers `[list]`; excludes `[exclusions]`. Add `[item]` for +`$[__]`.
13. Two options — Basic `$[__]` (`[narrow]`) / Standard `$[__]` (`[full]`). Which fits?
14. I can take it at `$[__]`. Deposit/full payment follows my gig terms. Once you confirm scope, I’ll share start time.
15. The tricky part is `[point]`. I can do a short review (~30 min) of your assets, then firm quote. OK to send files? (State if the review itself is free or paid.)

---

## D. Haggling (protect floor + offer alternatives)

16. Budget noted. Lowest I can go is `$[__]` with a smaller scope: `[what drops]`. Full scope stays at `$[__]`.
17. This already matches hours. Further cut means fewer rounds (e.g. 1) or a later delivery date — which works?
18. For tight budgets I offer **Basic** only — no rush, no heavy redesigns. I can’t match `$[their number]` on Standard.
19. Sorry — `$[their number]` is below my time cost and usually ends poorly for both. You’re welcome to Basic, or another seller.
20. I can move once to `$[__]` if scope stays frozen and feedback comes in one list. Below that I can’t take it.

---

## E. Rush

21. Rush possible if calendar allows. What’s the exact cutoff time? Rush fee `$[__]` or +`[__]%`, and I’d cap rounds at `[1–2]` so we don’t miss the clock.
22. Today’s full. Earliest start `[time]`. If that works, normal tier; if you need sooner, I’ll check whether a paid rush slot exists.
23. I can rush if you: (1) lock scope now (2) send all assets now (3) return feedback within `[X]` hours. If yes, we order.
24. For extreme urgency, I recommend a “minimum usable” version at `$[__]`; full polish later. Minimum usable or full?

---

## F. Declining (clear, short, no insults)

25. I can’t take this one: `[out of skill / risky use / calendar]`. Try `[one-line better direction]`. Good luck.
26. This use case is on my hard-no list (e.g. homework/exams / policy violations), so I won’t do it. Please use a compliant brief or another seller.
27. I still don’t have acceptance criteria. More chat without that burns both of us — write “done means ___” and come back, or pause.
28. I’m a better fit for `[my niche]`. Yours sounds more like `[their real need]`. I won’t take it rather than waste your time.

---

## G. Nudge / lock the order

29. Scope + quote are above. If you confirm, I’ll hold `[start / delivery]`. If the brief changes, send everything in one message.
30. Hi — I’m holding a slot until `[time]`. After that I may re-queue. Reply “confirm Standard” if you’re in.
31. If you have revision notes, please send a numbered list. If you’re still deciding, a quick “not yet” helps me free the slot.

---

## H. Endless questions, no order (polite filter)

32. I’ve answered the open questions. Next step is confirming **Standard at `$[__]`**. If you’re still comparing, no problem — ping me when decided so I’m not blocking calendar.
33. To stay fair to buyers waiting: after `[N]` consult rounds without a locked scope, I pause replies. When ready, send “confirmed scope + budget” and I’ll prioritize.
34. One last clarification: `[final point]`. If we still can’t lock it, please draft a short brief doc first — quotes get sharper that way.
35. Caution is fine. What I offer is clear scope, fixed rounds, and a dated delivery — not “do a free draft and pay if you like it” or unlimited edits. If that works, we continue; if not, all good.

---

## I. Delivery & revision stage (bonus)

36. First draft sent — please reply with ①②③ notes in one list. `[N]` revision rounds left on this order.
37. This note is a **scope change** (`[why]`), outside the original quote. Add-on `$[__]`; confirm to proceed, or I’ll finish within original scope.
38. Final files delivered as agreed: `[filename / format]`. Small fixes within `[X]` hours OK; after that or for new work, we treat it as a new order.

---

## Quick tips

- Offer choices (smaller scope / later date / other tier) instead of only pushing back.
- Decline with a reason, never mockery.
- Homework/exams, fake reviews, forgery → refuse; don’t bargain.
- Every `$` number must be **your** real floor — example figures are not promises.

*Scripts must match your real calendar and skills. No conversion-rate guarantee.*
